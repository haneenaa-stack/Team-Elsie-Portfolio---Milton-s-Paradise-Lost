Purpose, Audience & Constraints

Purpose of the Site: An atmospheric, interactive digital companion and collaborative essay repository designed to guide readers through the theological, psychological, and poetic labyrinth of John Milton’s Paradise Lost.

Target Audience: Students, literary scholars, and first-time readers of Milton’s Paradise Lost seeking visual orientation across the epic’s twelve books alongside peer-authored critical analyses.

Structural & Technical Constraints: Features 8 interactive apple nodes on a Renaissance Garden of Eden tree/maze landing hub—6 routing to individual Build 7 scholar workspaces (supporting new-window `_blank` launch) and 2 routing to collaborative Team Essays (Build 2 & Build 6), paired with a semantic `
